<section class="profile-intro">
  <p class="profile-intro__eyebrow">Inverse problems · Generative modeling · Model explainability</p>
  <p>I am currently a third-year PhD student in the <a href="https://www.stim.minesparis.psl.eu/en/home/">STIM</a> laboratory at Mines Paris - PSL and the <a href="https://lsp.dec.ens.fr/en">Laboratoire des Systèmes Perceptifs (LSP)</a> at École Normale Supérieure - PSL, under the supervision of <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=search_authors&amp;mauthors=Bruno+Figliuzzi">Bruno Figliuzzi</a> and <a href="https://lsp.dec.ens.fr/en/member/652/peter-neri">Peter Neri</a>.</p>
  <p>I am broadly interested in inverse problems, with my current work focusing more specifically on simplex-constrained inverse problems, especially in settings where observations can be modeled as mixtures or convex combinations of underlying components.</p>
  <p>My research initially focused on hyperspectral unmixing and has progressively broadened toward generative modeling, structured latent representations, and model explainability.</p>
  <div class="research-tags" aria-label="Research interests">
    <span>Inverse problems</span>
    <span>Simplex constraints</span>
    <span>Generative modeling</span>
    <span>Structured latent representations</span>
    <span>Model explainability</span>
  </div>
</section>
