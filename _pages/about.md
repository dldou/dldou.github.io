I am currently a third-year PhD student in applied mathematics at the [STIM](https://www.stim.minesparis.psl.eu/en/home/) laboratory, Mines Paris - PSL, and the Laboratoire des Systèmes Perceptifs (LSP), École Normale Supérieure - PSL, under the supervision of Bruno Figliuzzi and Peter Neri.

My research focuses on inverse problems and generative modeling. I first worked on hyperspectral unmixing, then on Dirichlet β-VAEs, where I identified a critical β-scale for posterior collapse. My interests now extend to recent developments in generative modeling, including models for discrete data, and their potential for solving challenging scientific inverse problems, particularly in biology and robotics.

Before starting my PhD, I completed an [M.Sc. in Automatic Control and Robotics](https://fc.sorbonne-universite.fr/nos-offres/master-automatique-robotique-parcours-ingenierie-des-systemes-intellignets-isi/) in the Intelligent Systems Engineering (ISI) track at Sorbonne Université, after earning a [B.Sc. in Mathematics](https://sciences.sorbonne-universite.fr/formation-sciences/offre-de-formation/licences/les-l2-l3-nos-huit-disciplines-de-licence/licence-0) from the same university.
